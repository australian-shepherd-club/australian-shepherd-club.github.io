Privacy Policy

Australian Shepherd Guide is a set of plain web pages served by GitHub Pages. You can read every herding, exercise and coat guide without signing up, and there is nothing here that asks for your name or email.

No cookies are set by the site. Your browser keeps a small reading counter and, on pages with tools, the values you type, in localStorage on your own device; none of it leaves your computer. GitHub runs the servers and may log technical data such as IP addresses, as its privacy statement explains.

Videos load from youtube-nocookie.com only when a page includes one, and outside links follow the rules of the sites they lead to. Something unclear? Get in touch.
